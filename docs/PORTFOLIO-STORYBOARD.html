A cursor you can feel

The pointer is the degree mark from the wordmark. It wipes glass, bends isobars, magnifies work, drags the strip and pulls buttons. Touch receives its own equivalent, not a degraded hover.

Scroll as a timeline

Pins are short and always show progress. Every pinned scene has a clear start and end state, and the nav's degree marker shows the section.

The work stays loudest

Studio chrome is monochrome type; color comes from the project in view. Every concept is shown on desktop and phone, playing its own signature interaction, because all 25 have polished mobile builds.

Fast, then fancy

Posters paint first; WebGL and video load behind them. One shared canvas, capped pixel ratio, only the active video plays. Reduced motion gets a complete static page.

01 · ARRIVAL

The fogged window

The whole first screen is a pane of condensation. Behind it, the 25 concepts drift past as blurred color. The cursor wipes the glass clear; type that falls inside the wipe flips to white; beads run from the edges and the fog slowly returns.

Pointer
Wipe width follows pointer speed. The cleared area holds for about 3 s, then condenses again. Idle for 2 s: a “ghost finger” draws one short stroke to teach the gesture.
Behind
Short clips of each concept doing its signature interaction (smoke following a pointer, a plotter drawing, a bench assembling) crossfade every ~5 s under the fog; the ticks show which. Clicking the label opens that project.
Build
WebGL: blurred and sharp textures of the current project; a ping-pong wipe mask (pointer strokes, decaying); a droplet normal map that refracts. Headline is real DOM text with a masked white twin.
Fallback
Reduced motion or no WebGL: one sharp poster behind a static CSS frost, a pre-drawn wipe across the middle. Copy and actions are identical.

02 · TRANSITION

Clearing the glass

One pinned screen. Scrolling clears the fog, then the project behind it flies into the next sentence. The first scroll pays off with a visible transformation.

Scroll
Pinned for 1 viewport, scrubbed (no autoplay). Scrolling back reverses it exactly.
Motion
FLIP from full-bleed to the chip's measured box; corner radius and the montage crossfade freeze at the current project.
Fallback
No pin. The poster sits above the statement with a normal reveal.

04 · SELECTED WORK · CENTREPIECE

The forecast

Six projects, one pinned screen each. As a project arrives, a weather front sweeps across and the whole page takes on its atmosphere: background, type color, grain, light. Each project gets a one-line forecast instead of a generic blurb.

Scroll
Pinned ≈ 0.9 viewport per project with a short hold, so a project can be read at rest. Snaps gently to rest points; it never jumps.
Pointer
The capture tilts toward the cursor; a refractive lens magnifies under it; the cursor becomes a “View” disc. Its silhouette blurs with pointer speed.
Media
Two muted 6–8 s loops per project, recorded doing its signature interaction: desktop in the tilted frame, phone in the device beside it, parallaxing at a different depth. Posters first; only the active pair plays.
Copy
The forecast line is the playful hook; “Try in the concept” states the real interaction, taken from docs/style.md.
Front
WebGL wipe with a refraction band at the edge, timed to scroll. DOM text swaps behind it with a line-mask rise.
Fallback
Six stacked cards with their atmosphere color, no pin.

09 · /WORK/

The index as a weather map

All 25 as a large typographic list, each with its signature interaction. The hovered row brightens, a preview follows the cursor and leans with its velocity, and the page tints toward that project's atmosphere.

Pointer
Preview follows with spring lag; it plays the interaction clip after 400 ms of rest. “Phone view” swaps every preview for the mobile build.
Columns
Number, name, style, and the signature interaction in one line, so the list reads as 25 different ideas, not 25 thumbnails.
Filters
Counts, keyboard and screen-reader state, kept in the URL and restored on Back. Rows animate out and in (FLIP) when the filter changes.
Touch
Becomes a column of cards with posters; no hover-dependent content.

11 · STORY · LIVE DEVICE

Play it here

Every story page lets the visitor use the actual concept inside a device frame, phone first. It is the strongest proof of the two things all 25 share: their own interactions and a polished mobile build.

Load
Nothing loads until “Play” is pressed (poster with a play badge). Then a same-origin iframe of /work/<slug>/ at 390 × 844, scaled to fit; Desktop switches it to 1440 × 900.
Scope
One live frame per page, unloaded when it leaves the viewport. The index and landing never run live concepts.
Check
Verify each concept inside an iframe (sound, pointer capture, fixed headers, scroll pins) before enabling it for that project; fall back to clips if one misbehaves.
Access
Titled iframe, “Open full screen” link beside it, Esc returns focus to the page.

MOBILE

Same weather, smaller sky

The studio site has to be as polished on a phone as the 25 concepts are. Every scene keeps its idea, with shorter pins and touch-native gestures. Nothing important hides behind hover.

Scroll
Native scroll on touch devices (Lenis desktop only). Pins at most 1 viewport; horizontal strip becomes swipe with snap.
Perf
Canvas at DPR ≤ 1.5, posters under 120 KB, clips only on Wi-Fi-class connections or tap.
