01 / The brief

The imagined subject

Self-initiated concept. A garden-planning app concept with an actual browser-local bed planner, seasonal visualization, plan comparison, and waitlist.

The visitor

People exploring a small-garden planning app concept for a balcony or yard.

The design question

How can a landing page let visitors try the very plan it is describing?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Let the visitor make the plan

Drag, touch placement and keyboard controls place, move or remove plants. Overlays, spread rings and spacing notes respond to the arrangement rather than sitting beside a canned demo.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Keep the edited garden alive

The axonometric seasonal view uses the visitor’s own bed. Season and hemisphere controls continue the same plan into a different view, while the sketchbook language ties the tool to the rest of the page.

Phone capture
