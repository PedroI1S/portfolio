03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Make the first encounter edible

The box arrives with illustrated snacks bursting out. Tapping a snack crumbles it, introducing the same small physical jokes that appear throughout the page.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Explain the trip through one object

A scroll sequence selects Mexico, packs the snacks and closes the box. The box becomes a postcard stamp, connecting the subscription steps to the featured country.

Phone capture

Decision / 03

Keep the details easy to inspect

The country postcard flips, crunch meters animate and billing controls update displayed prices. FAQ and newsletter validation provide local states around the illustrated story.

Desktop capture

04 / Scope & credits

Concept, design and build by otherweather, with original SVG snack and mascot illustrations. The subscription, plans, prices, products and allergen details are illustrative. No snacks are sold or shipped.
