01 / The brief

The imagined subject

An exhibition website for a fictional photographer's retrospective, showing eight works, captions, an archive index, and visitor information.

The visitor

Visitors browsing an imagined photographer’s retrospective and exhibition information.

The design question

How can the act of revealing a photograph shape the pace of looking at it?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Develop the image in stages

Each print appears with highlights before shadows. Varied scale and placement make the eight-work walk feel like an exhibition rather than a uniform gallery.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Give the visitor the light

A shared-element transition takes a print into the dark room. Spotlight, full-light, keyboard and touch controls offer several ways to inspect the same image; List/Grid provides a separate collection view.

Phone capture

04 / Scope & credits
