01 / The brief

The imagined subject

Self-initiated concept. A quarterly publication's website introducing an issue about cities at walking pace, its articles, contributors, and subscription.

The visitor

Readers interested in everyday city life, public space and independent magazines.

The design question

How can the structure of a publication borrow the pace and discoveries of a walk?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Turn contents into places

Five departments open beneath scalloped awnings. The After Hours control changes the same street to night, giving the table of contents a setting of its own.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Let the feature occupy a whole block

The Long Block moves a panorama past a fold line, distance counter and station captions. A directly scrollable static version preserves the walk when the animated treatment is unavailable.
