01 / The brief

The imagined subject

Self-initiated concept. A corporate group website organizing four workwear/outdoor/safety/textile brands, operations, investor information, news, careers, and responsibility.

The visitor

Investors, partners and job seekers browsing an imagined group of four industrial brands.

The design question

How can a broad corporate group explain its purpose through one understandable product?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Give the corporate story an object

One pair, three lives assembles a boot from material panels and later replaces the outsole. The repaired boot carries the narrative into responsibility, making product life the connecting idea.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Keep a large organization navigable

A Group mega-menu and single-open brand panels organize four businesses. Period controls and keyboard inspection make the illustrative investor chart browsable alongside news and careers.
