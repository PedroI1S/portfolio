01 / The brief

The imagined subject

A mountain-lodge website introducing a fictional forty-room retreat, its approach route, rooms, sauna/lake rituals, seasons, table, and booking enquiry.

The visitor

Travellers exploring an imagined lodge, its rooms, rituals and seasons.

The design question

How can a hotel website give arrival a sense of place before showing a room?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Make the arrival continuous

An altitude count lands in the hero. The valley darkens, windows warm and the wordmark unthreads as the camera approaches; amber light carries the visitor into the paper page.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Let climate organize the stay

A shuttle map and draggable rooms establish the visit. Sauna and lake temperatures change the grade, while a single Summer/Winter switch lowers a ridge-shaped snow line over the landscape.

Phone capture
