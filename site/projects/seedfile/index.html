03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Show the drawing happen

A preflight and homing sequence precedes the first mark. Reseed and skip controls let visitors choose between watching the process and reaching the work.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Use one pen throughout

The same drawing engine supplies the hero, scroll reel, ledger previews and service sketches. Its changing job readout makes the visible workshop structure part of the identity.
