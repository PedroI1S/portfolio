01 / The brief

The imagined subject

A campaign for fictional rail operator Linha Atlântica, presenting an overnight Atlantic-coast journey to Porto through five stops and an invented upgrade reward.

The visitor

Travellers following a fictional rail campaign along the Atlantic coast.

The design question

How can a ticket carry the visitor’s progress through a scrolling journey?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Let one object remember the trip

The opening ticket is punched and torn; its stub becomes the stamp card. Passing the chapter ranges collects stamps even during fast scrolling, then reissues the completed ticket in gold.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Change the view with every stop

Painted scenes use distinct window silhouettes, shutters and camera depth. The timetable and optional procedural sound extend the railway setting around the chapters.

Phone capture

04 / Scope & credits

Concept, design and build by otherweather. Painted scenes are AI-generated illustrations. Linha Atlântica, Sleeper No. 7, the through-route, timetable, fares and upgrade are invented; nothing can be booked.

Portfolio concept by Pedro / otherweather. Design, creative development, motion and responsive implementation.
