01 / The brief

The imagined subject

A fictional one-person 3D studio's playable portfolio, where a tiny planet's landmarks represent interactive projects.

The visitor

Curious visitors and potential collaborators exploring an imagined one-person 3D studio.

The design question

How can a portfolio communicate its craft by letting visitors play with the work?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Make navigation spatial

Dragging or orbiting reveals the planet; choosing a place flies the camera to its landmark and changes the light. The menu and illustrated list offer direct routes to the same projects.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Give every stop a small purpose

The lighthouse uncovers logbook lines, the cottage passes through twelve months and the windmill grinds flour. Tide and tree-growth controls turn the other landmarks into working scenes.

Phone capture

Decision / 03

Keep the world self-contained

Procedural models, sky and optional sound create the diorama in the browser. A reset control lets exploration begin again without needing an avatar or a score.

Desktop capture

04 / Scope & credits

Concept, design and build by otherweather. Models, sky and sound are generated in the browser with Three.js r170, without image or audio files. Type: Playfair Display, Lato and IBM Plex Mono. The studio, artist, clients and projects are fictional.
