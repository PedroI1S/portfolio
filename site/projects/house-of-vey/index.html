01 / The brief

The imagined subject

Self-initiated concept. An accessories-house collection launch introducing four bags through a walk-through townhouse and a final collection grid.

The visitor

Visitors discovering the first collection of an imagined accessories house.

The design question

How can a collection have the intimacy and continuity of visiting a home?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Make the threshold a real transition

The opening arch leads into the front door as the facade and lamps light up. Repeated arches keep the gate, room passages and final window part of one journey.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Let the product inhabit the scene

Each room gives one bag its own proportion, color and light. Hotspots open detail panels, and swatches recolor both the panel image and the bag inside the room.

Phone capture

Decision / 03

Open the house into the collection

The bedroom window expands into the final collection grid. The narrative reaches a straightforward product view without abandoning the architectural motif.

Desktop capture

04 / Scope & credits

Concept, design and build by otherweather. Bags are AI-generated renders of fictional products; prices, dates and addresses are illustrative, and nothing is sold. Optional sound is generated in the browser and starts off.
