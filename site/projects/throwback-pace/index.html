03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Give the print language momentum

Stickers can be dragged and tossed on desktop. Scroll velocity drives rewind and fast-forward cues, while directional image wipes give the page a stepped campaign rhythm.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Run the track through the shop

The opening blue track becomes a frame around the product row. Colorway swatches, distance filters and route drawings connect campaign graphics to working local shopping and club states.
