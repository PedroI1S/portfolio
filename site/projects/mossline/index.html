01 / The brief

The imagined subject

Self-initiated concept. A soil-carbon monitoring concept explaining buried probes, measurement models, reports, plot readings, and regenerative-farming applications.

The visitor

Farmers and project partners exploring an imagined soil-carbon monitoring system.

The design question

How can a buried measurement process become visible without losing the life above it?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Start with the living ground

Plant growth and a gently swaying specimen establish the subject before any chart appears. A small pointer-driven frame nudge keeps the rendered object responsive.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Make the cut explain the instrument

Blender-rendered image sequences turn toward the cut face, descend through the horizons and place the probe. Three bands align with depth labels and readouts. The delivered cut uses discrete frames; the hero sway blends.

Phone capture

Decision / 03

Carry the story into data

A five-season chart and illustrated field map extend the specimen into plot-level readings. Plot controls and keyboard tooltips make the illustrative measurements explorable.

Desktop capture

04 / Scope & credits

Concept, design and build by otherweather. The specimen is rendered in Blender Cycles and delivered as desktop and mobile image sequences. Farms, plots and readings are illustrative. Mossline is fictional and sells no sensors, carbon credits or verification services.

Portfolio concept by Pedro / otherweather. Design, creative development, motion and responsive implementation.
