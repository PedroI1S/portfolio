03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Give latency a visible sequence

Dormant ribbons ignite alongside an illustrative 0.84-second clock and three milestones. The field continues through the page, resolving into a dawn horizon at the end.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Pair spectacle with useful controls

The command palette, globe regions, code tabs and copy actions make the product story navigable. A calculator lets visitors change GPU class, request duration and daily volume to explore illustrative estimates.
