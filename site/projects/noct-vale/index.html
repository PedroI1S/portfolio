01 / The brief

The imagined subject

Self-initiated concept. A perfume house's anniversary website presenting current scents, bottle details, ingredient histories, and a fifteen-year archive.

The visitor

Fragrance enthusiasts exploring an imagined house and its scent histories.

The design question

How can a screen give each scent an atmosphere of its own?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Give scent a visible presence

A real-time smoke field responds to the pointer. Each scent changes the photograph, notes, page tint and smoke palette, while vial indicators keep the sequence legible.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Use light to reveal detail

A close view of the bottle catches its engraving with a light sweep. Filtered archive entries and smoke-dissolve previews repeat the same restrained reveal at a smaller scale.

Phone capture
