01 / The brief

The imagined subject

Self-initiated concept. A closed-loop geothermal energy concept explaining the system, pilot sites, industrial/district-heating applications, and careers.

The visitor

Industrial and district-heating teams exploring an imagined geothermal system.

The design question

How can the route of energy underground become a story a visitor can follow?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Establish the surface first

The wellhead rises through the horizon and pilot maps draw their heat mains. These sites give the underground journey a destination before the descent begins.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Follow both sides of the loop

Depth, strata, casing and water temperatures change together as the visitor follows cold and hot legs. Returning to the surface becomes the careers sunrise, closing the same visual circuit.

Phone capture

04 / Scope & credits

Concept, design and build by otherweather. Undersun is fictional: sites, figures, people and roles are illustrative. It does not drill wells, sell heat or power, or offer investments.
