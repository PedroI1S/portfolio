01 / The brief

The imagined subject

Self-initiated concept. A private-credit firm's website explaining patient five-to-seven-year lending, its approach, facilities, and contact desks.

The visitor

Founders exploring the imagined firm’s approach to long-term lending.

The design question

How can the time a business needs to grow become visible on a financial website?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Make time tangible

The red full stop becomes a company point. As the visitor scrolls, contour rings grow around it while the year and illustrative lending figures advance together.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Keep the details close

Strategy bars reveal image plates on desktop and open as touch accordions. The same spare grid carries the quieter facilities, facts and contact information.

Phone capture
