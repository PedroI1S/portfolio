01 / The brief

The imagined subject

An independent type foundry's website for exploring fictional font families, testing type, and comparing licence options.

The visitor

Designers exploring fictional font families and testing their own words.

The design question

How can browsing a type collection feel as direct as working with the type itself?

03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Make the specimen adjustable

Pointer position drives width and weight in the hero Plot, with rulers and live values exposing the axes. The letterform is something the visitor can handle.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Connect exploration to use

The spatial gallery supports drag, pause and keyboard selection. Choosing a card transfers its family into the editable tester, linking an expressive discovery mode to a practical proof.

Phone capture

04 / Scope & credits

Concept, design and build by otherweather. Fictional font families use real SIL Open Font License fonts: Archivo (Omnibus-Type), Fraunces (Undercase Type), Space Grotesk (Florian Karsten), Instrument Serif (Rodrigo Fuenzalida and Jordan Egstad), Bricolage Grotesque (Mathieu Triay), Martian Mono (Roman Shamin and Evil Martians), Newsreader (Production Type), Big Shoulders (Patric King) and Inter Tight (Rasmus Andersson). Family names, prices and licences presented by the fictional foundry are illustrative; nothing is sold.

Portfolio concept by Pedro / otherweather. Design, creative development, motion and responsive implementation.
