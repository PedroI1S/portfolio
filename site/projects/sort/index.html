03 / Decisions that shape the experience

Decision / 01

Give the logo a job

Letter controls change the wordmark’s order. A travelling rule highlight carries the chosen mode down into a corresponding product-grid arrangement, connecting identity to browsing.

Desktop capture

Decision / 02

Keep commerce tactile

Paired photographs reveal front and detail views. Quick view, size selection and local add/remove bag states make each object inspectable, while the page clearly identifies the shop as a concept.

Phone capture

Decision / 03

Let type carry the graphic system

Variable-width lettering, offset columns, archive treatments and a velocity-reactive marquee extend the same cobalt-on-putty language beyond the opening wordmark.

Desktop capture

04 / Scope & credits

Concept, design and build by otherweather. Products are AI-generated photographs of objects that do not exist. Prices, weights and workshops are illustrative; the shopping bag is local and never checks out.
