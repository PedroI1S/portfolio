17:00 — Practice

We draw slowly, build exactly and leave the rest to the light.

Cal & Sombra was founded in 2009 by two architects who met while repairing a lime-washed village school in Trás-os-Montes. The name is still the brief we give ourselves: lime for the surfaces, shadow for everything else.

Eighteen of us work from a former rope workshop in Miragaia: architects, interior designers, a restoration specialist, and a model maker whose study models line every wall.

18:00 — Services

Four ways of working, one drawing table.

  1. 01

    Architecture

    New houses, small public buildings and workplaces, from the first site visit to the last snag list.

    Typical scale: 90 ⟷to 2,500 m²

  2. 02

    Interiors

    Rooms, joinery and lighting, drawn at 1:20 and built with the same four workshops every time.

    Typical scale: 20 ⟷to 600 m²

  3. 03

    Masterplanning

    Village and campus plans that begin with what is already there: walls, paths, trees and shade.

    Typical scale: 1 ⟷to 40 ha

  4. 04

    Restoration

    Granite, lime and timber repaired by their original methods, with new parts that are honest about being new.

    Typical scale: 40 ⟷to 3,000 m²
