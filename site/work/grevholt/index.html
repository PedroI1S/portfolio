03 Material, build, repair

One pair,
three lives.

Upper 2.2 mm full-grain, own tannery

Toe cap Double-stitched

Goodyear welt Stitched, not glued

  1. 01 Material

    A hide, a reel of thread, a sole.

    Every Oakhand boot starts in our own tannery and our Selvane mill: 2.2 mm full-grain leather, waxed cotton lining and bonded nylon thread rated to 40 newtons.

  2. 02 Build

    Forty-one parts. One welt.

    Cut, closed and lasted by twelve pairs of hands. The sole is stitched to a leather welt instead of glued to the upper, which is what makes the third life possible.

    • 1Upper 2.2 mm full-grain leather from our own tannery
    • 2Toe cap Double-stitched over the vamp
    • 3Goodyear welt Stitched, not glued, so the sole can be replaced

  3. 03 Repair

    Nine years on, a new sole.

    Send them back and our workshop cuts the worn sole away, keeps the welt and stitches on a new one. Same boot, another decade.

    61,000 pairs resoled in 2025
