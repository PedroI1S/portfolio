A passage along the Atlantic · Summer 2027

prologue · platform 3, 19:30

Board in Bilbao as the light goes, eat late while the sea runs beside the window, sleep through the long dark middle of the coast, and wake to coffee in the vineyards an hour younger than you left.

Five stops, five stamps. Your card in the corner fills as the journey goes; collect all five and the end of the line has a first-class upgrade waiting.

03Ribadeoarr. 00:52 · dep. 01:00

RIBADEO

00:52 · HALT 8 MIN

eight minutesof Quiet

The only stop the whole night. The engine ticks as it cools, a moth finds the lantern, someone in car 6 steps down in socks to look at the stars. Then a door, a whistle, and the coast again.

Stretch your legs if you're awake. The attendant knocks once for anyone who asked to see the halt.

At Valença the train crosses the river Minho into Portugal and every watch on board goes back an hour. You wake at the same time and somehow it's earlier. The tray arrives with the sun.

Back to the title card

Linha Atlântica is a fictional company created as a website concept by Pedro. The operator, Sleeper No. 7, its timetable, fares, upgrade codes and contact details are invented; nothing here can be booked, and no such through service runs on this coast. The painted scenes are AI-generated illustrations made for this concept. One real fact: mainland Portugal keeps clock time one hour behind Spain, so the clocks do go back at the border.
