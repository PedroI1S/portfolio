01 The loop

One sealed well. A town’s worth of heat, for fifty years.

We drill a single closed loop into hot rock and run water through it. No fracking, no groundwater drawn, nothing burned. The ground does the work, day and night, in every season.

03 How it works

Water goes down cold and comes up hot. Nothing else leaves the ground.

  1. 01Drill once

    Two legs go down from a single pad and meet 4,800 metres below, joined by sealed laterals that run through rock at around 150 °C.

  2. 02Close the loop

    Water with a corrosion inhibitor circulates inside steel-lined pipe. It never touches the rock: no fracking, no groundwater drawn, nothing vented.

  3. 03Let it rise

    Hot water is lighter than cold. That difference keeps the loop turning by itself, so the circulation pumps idle at a fraction of their rating.

  4. 04Hand off the heat

    A surface exchanger feeds a district network, a factory’s process line or a small turbine for power. The water cools, goes back down, and the loop starts again.
