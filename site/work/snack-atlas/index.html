Issue 14 · October 2026

This month: Mexico

Six favourites from Mexico City's corner tiendas and market stalls, sorted loudest to quietest by our crunch-o-meter.

  • Chicharrones de harina

    Puffed wheat wheels with lime and hot sauce. Eat over a plate.

    10/10 · deafening crunch

  • Cacahuates japoneses

    Peanuts in a crisp soy-glazed shell. Invented in Mexico City, despite the name.

    9/10 · loud crunch

  • Palanqueta

    Peanut brittle bar set in piloncillo, the dark cane sugar.

    8/10 · snappy crunch

  • Pepitas con chile y limón

    Toasted pumpkin seeds, chilli and lime. Dangerously handful-sized.

    6/10 · polite crunch

  • Obleas con cajeta

    Paper-thin wafers sandwiching goat's milk caramel.

    3/10 · whisper crunch

  • Mazapán de cacahuate

    Pressed peanut candy that crumbles if you look at it. Unwrap with care.

    1/10 · silent crunch
