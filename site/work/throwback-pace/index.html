[03] Since ’94, give or take

Eleven people, one borrowed stopwatch.

Throwback Pace Co. started on the cinder track behind a swimming baths that had already closed. Nobody was fast. Everybody stayed for chips. Thirty-odd years on, the stopwatch still works, the club runs in nine cities, and the only rule hasn’t changed: the run isn’t over until the last person is in.

Lap ’94 is the kit we wore that first winter, redrawn with better zips.
