( What we make )

Six things, all of them made for the evening scroll.

Pick one or take the lot. Every brief starts with the same question: what would make you stop, at 9 pm, on the sofa, with the TV on?

  1. We find the hour, the format and the joke your audience is already having, then write the plan around it. One page, not forty.

    In the box
    Audience listening, channel plan, a monthly content calendar
    Takes
    3 weeks

  2. Scripted, shot and cut for the first second, because the first second is the only one anybody promises you.

    In the box
    Hooks and scripts, shoot days, edits in 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1
    Takes
    10 days a batch

  3. We cast creators who already talk like your customers, then give them a brief short enough to remember without notes.

    In the box
    Casting, briefs and usage rights, paid amplification
    Takes
    2 to 6 weeks

  4. A drop, a release, a reopening. We plan the first 48 hours down to the minute and stay up for all of them.

    In the box
    Countdown content, a launch-night room, a paid boost plan
    Takes
    4 to 8 weeks

  5. Somebody has to answer the comments. We do it in your voice, at the hours people actually comment.

    In the box
    A voice guide, daily moderation, a monthly “what they said” report
    Takes
    Ongoing

  6. Streams, live drops and on-the-ground coverage, run by a crew that has done it before and has a plan B taped to the camera.

    In the box
    Run of show, crew and kit, clips cut while it is still live
    Takes
    One night, two weeks of prep

( What we believe )

Turn it up ↓

Nobody opens an app to see an ad. They open it at 9 pm, on the sofa, half listening to somebody else, looking for one thing worth sending to a friend. That is the loud hour. Everything we make is built to win it: a first second that earns the next one, a joke that survives the mute button, a reply that sounds like a person wrote it. Small things, made for the loudest part of the day.

That is theloud hour.
