A playable portfolio

Thimble Planet

I build small, warm worlds for brands, museums and festivals: idents, product films, browser games and real-time pieces. This site is usually a tiny planet you can orbit. Here it is laid flat, one place per project.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so the planet is shown as a list.

This browser can’t draw the 3D planet, so here it is as a list.

You’re reading the list version.

  1. 01 The Cottage

    Slow Mornings

    Year
    2025
    Role
    Modelling, lighting, animation
    For
    An independent tea blender

    Twelve ten-second idents, one for each month, all set in the same small kitchen while the light outside changes. Every curl of steam was simulated, then trimmed by hand.

  2. 02 The Windmill

    Stone-ground

    Year
    2024
    Role
    Art direction, product film
    For
    An organic flour mill

    A ninety-second film that follows a single grain from the field to a loaf, all at toy scale. The millstones have forty faces each and turn at the real mill’s speed.

  3. 03 The Dock

    Low Tide Regatta

    Year
    2023
    Role
    Game design, 3D, code
    For
    A small-boat sailing festival

    A one-thumb boat race that lived on the festival’s ticket page. Visitors sailed forty thousand races over the opening weekend, and the record still stands at 41 seconds.

  4. 04 The Lighthouse

    Keeper’s Log

    Year
    2022
    Role
    Real-time 3D, WebGL build
    For
    A harbour museum’s centenary

    Visitors turn the lamp by hand, and each sweep of the beam lights up one entry from a keeper’s logbook: a hundred years of weather in a browser tab.

  5. 05 The Grove

    Thirty Small Forests

    Year
    2021
    Role
    Everything (a personal project)
    For
    Myself, one evening at a time

    A new miniature forest every day for a month, each one built in under two hours with fewer than a thousand triangles. This planet grew out of it.

About this site

Thimble Planet is a one-person 3D studio. Commissions are open for spring 2027: write to hello@thimbleplanet.example.

The planet is drawn live with Three.js r170 and plain JavaScript. Every model, the sky and the sound are made in the browser; there are no image or audio files. Type is set in Playfair Display, Lato and IBM Plex Mono.
