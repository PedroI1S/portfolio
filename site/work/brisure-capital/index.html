03 — Strategies

Four ways in.

Facility size, €m 050100

  1. One senior facility for buyouts and growth, replacing a stack of lenders with a single counterparty.

    Facility size €15–80m

    Typical use: a buyout or a second site. Senior secured, 5 to 7 years, one agent and one set of documents.

  2. Lending against receivables, inventory and equipment, sized to the collateral rather than the forecast.

    Facility size €10–50m

    Typical use: working capital that grows with the order book. Borrowing base reset monthly against eligible assets.

  3. Refinancing, shareholder buy-outs and succession, for the moment a family business changes hands.

    Facility size €10–60m

    Typical use: buying out a retiring shareholder without selling the company. Repayment shaped around the handover.

  4. The margin steps down as measured targets are met, verified by a third party every year.

    Facility size €15–70m

    Typical use: capex for energy and emissions targets. Up to 25 basis points off the margin, tested once a year.
