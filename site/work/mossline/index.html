  1. [ 01 / sensor ]

    Listen to the living soil.

    A forearm-length probe reads moisture, temperature and respiration at three depths. Once an hour. For five years.

  2. [ 02 / model ]

    Find the carbon in the noise.

    Readings meet lab cores, ground cover and your grazing log. A plot-level estimate, with its uncertainty left in.

  3. [ 03 / verified report ]

    Every reading leaves a trace.

    Fresh cores check the model each September. Every tonne traces back to a plot, a probe and a lab sample.

04. who it’s for

Better ground. Common ground.

  • [ farmers ]

    Get paid for the carbon you actually build.

    One morning to install, no sampling days lost. You see every plot in one dashboard, and the data stays yours if you ever leave.

    • Installed in a morning
    • Five-year battery
    • Your data, your export

  • [ buyers ]

    Buy removals with the receipts attached.

    Every tonne in a Mossline report links to the plot, the probe and the lab core behind it, and you can open each one yourself.

    • Plot-level provenance
    • Annual lab verification
    • 20 % held in a buffer pool

  • [ auditors ]

    Check the claim without a site visit.

    • Raw data as CSV or Parquet
    • Versioned, documented model
    • Signed season hashes
