prologue

Kamen is forty rooms of stone and charred larch on a south-facing shelf above the Tihadol valley, built for people who want to arrive tired and leave slower: to swim in a lake that stays cold all summer, sit in a sauna that is never quite full, eat at one long table with whoever came up the same road, and remember, by the second morning, what an unscheduled day feels like.

chapter 01 · arrival

the road runs out where the Larches begin

  1. 55 minFrom Ljubljana airport to Tihadol village on the valley road. Leave the car in our barn by the river.
  2. 9 kmThe last stretch is ours: a larch-lined forest road, an electric shuttle every hour, no cars past the gate.
  3. 1,140 mTea by the fire and a key on a leather loop. Check-in is a conversation, not a counter.

chapter 03 · sauna & lake

heat, then the Lake, then nothing at all

  1. I.

    Heat

    A wood-fired sauna at the end of the jetty, 85 °C, birch whisks on the bench. Twelve minutes is plenty.

  2. II.

    Cold

    Down the ladder into the lake: 14 °C in August, 4 °C in March, glass-still at dawn.

  3. III.

    Rest

    A 36 °C foot bath, wool blankets on the deck, a jug of pine-tip water and nothing to read. Repeat twice.

chapter 04 · seasons

the same house in two Weathers

Snow line 2,600 m

Summer. Snow line above 2,600 metres.

From the summit to our door: follow the snow line.

June – September

The lake opens on the first of June. Ridge walks leave at seven, wild strawberries line the larch road, and dinner moves to the terrace until the light gives out around nine.

December – March

Ski touring from the door with a guide, snowshoes on loan, a hole cut in the lake ice beside the sauna. The shuttle runs on chains and every window glows by four.

chapter 05 · table

one long table for the whole Valley

Miza seats twenty-eight at a single oak table. One sitting at half past seven, one menu, written each afternoon from what the valley farms sent up the road.
