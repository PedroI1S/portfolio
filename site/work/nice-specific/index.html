p. 08 — The detail pass

Client brief, received 9 Jan: “There’s nothing interesting here.”

Brimble Hydraulics Ltd

Delivering Excellence in Fluid Control Since 1971

Company Overview 2025 · Rev. 7 · Page 3 of 14

Brimble Hydraulics is a leading provider of innovative fluid-control solutions for a wide range of industrial sectors. We are committed to excellence, quality and customer satisfaction in everything we do. Our experienced team works in partnership with clients to understand their requirements and deliver solutions that add value across the entire product lifecycle.

Founded in 1971, the company has grown steadily to become a trusted name in the design, manufacture and supply of valves, actuators and associated components. Today we operate from a modern facility in the West Midlands and serve customers across the UK, Europe and beyond.

Our product range includes gate valves, globe valves, check valves, ball valves and bespoke assemblies, available in a variety of sizes, pressure ratings and materials to suit the needs of each application.

Our people are our greatest asset. Many of our staff have been with the company for over twenty years, bringing a wealth of knowledge and experience to every project we undertake.

Quality is at the heart of our operations. All products are manufactured to the relevant standards and undergo rigorous testing before leaving our facility. Our values are integrity, innovation, partnership and sustainability. We hold a number of accreditations and continually review our processes to drive improvement.

We understand that downtime is costly. That is why our products are designed for reliability and ease of maintenance, supported by a comprehensive range of spare parts and a responsive technical team.

Brimble valves are installed at sites across the food and drink, water, energy and chemical industries. In one instance, a valve in a Speyside distillery ran from 1984 to 2024 without a service, which demonstrates the durability of our engineering. Further case studies are available on request.

Health and safety is a priority at every level of the business, and we maintain a strong record across all of our sites.

Our service offering includes installation support, on-site inspection, refurbishment and planned maintenance contracts, tailored to the operational requirements of each customer. We pride ourselves on going the extra mile for every client.

Sustainability is increasingly important to our customers and to us. We are working to reduce energy use across our operations, minimise waste and source materials responsibly, in line with our environmental policy.

Looking ahead, we will continue to invest in our people, our facilities and our product development capabilities, so that we remain well placed to meet the evolving needs of the market. For further information, please contact our sales team.

p. 34 — How it goes

How it goes, in three honest steps.

  1. Step 01 · Weeks 1–2

    Snoop

    Two weeks of interviews, ride-alongs and early mornings in the support queue. We read the complaint emails and the old brochures, looking for the detail nobody in your marketing team thinks is interesting. It is always the interesting one.

  2. Step 02 · Weeks 3–8

    Make

    Writers, directors and producers work from the same notes, in the same room. You see first drafts and rough cuts early, while changes are still cheap and nobody is attached to anything.

  3. Step 03 · Month 3 onward

    Keep going

    We publish on a schedule your team can actually keep, then hand over the playbook, the templates and every frame of raw footage. It is all yours. We just like to be asked back.

Typical first project: 8–10 weeks, from £18,000. Retainers from month three.
