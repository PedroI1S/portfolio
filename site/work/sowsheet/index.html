02 · Features

Three sheets, that's it!

Everything a small garden needs, nothing it doesn't.

  1. 1

    Tape measure optional!

    Draw your plot

    Pace it out, trace a photo or type the sizes. Balconies, yards, a single windowsill: if it holds soil, it fits on a sheet.

  2. 2

    No more guessing!

    Follow the sun

    Sowsheet works out the sun hours on every square from your street, your fences and the date, then redraws the shade month by month.

  3. 3

    Gentle nudges only!

    Know when to sow

    A sowing calendar built from your plan and your local frost dates. One reminder a week, never a lecture.

03 · Seasons

Same plot, four moods!

One plan, through the year.

That bed you just made? Here it is, growing up. Turn the season to see your seedlings fill out, fruit and settle down for a rest.

A plan isn't a still life. Follow this little garden through four seasons, from the first shoots to a well-earned rest.

Same squares. Same plants. An illustrative growth preview, not a local sowing calendar.

  1. You are here

    Spring Mar–May

    Sow lettuce in the shady end. Blossom on the apple; frost still possible, so keep a fleece handy.

  2. You are here

    Summer Jun–Aug

    Tomatoes ripen against the warm fence. Water at dawn, and pick before the birds do.

  3. You are here

    Autumn Sep–Nov

    Pumpkins in, kale holding on. Rake the leaves straight onto the bed; they're next year's soil.

  4. You are here

    Winter Dec–Feb

    The bed rests under a blanket. You plan next year on paper, with a cup of tea and a robin for company.
