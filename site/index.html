04 / How the weather changes

From a first thought
to out in the world.

DirectionDesignBuildLaunch

  1. 01 / Direction

    Find the atmosphere.

    A conversation about your audience, ambitions and what the website needs to do.

    You receive: A clear brief and creative direction.

  2. 02 / Design

    Give the idea a shape.

    Typography, composition and motion studies become a coherent visual language.

    You receive: A responsive design to build from.

  3. 03 / Build

    Make it feel alive.

    Design becomes a working website. Interaction, movement and responsive behavior are developed together.

    You receive: A working website on every device.

  4. 04 / Refine & launch

    Every last degree.

    Test the experience across devices, refine the details, and prepare the website for launch.

    You receive: A launched site and a clear handover.
